# 0005 — Serenix's own score names and scales

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Status:** accepted

Composite scores are named Sleep Score, Readiness and Load (not WHOOP's Sleep Performance,
Recovery and Strain), and all run 0–100 rather than copying WHOOP's 0–21 strain scale. They
are our own algorithms, and borrowing WHOOP's branded names or scales would invite trademark
complaints to Apple and suggest an equivalence we can't claim.
